One dot, seven states

Chief of Staff, readingBreathes while it reads new events
RunningGreen, a ring ripples out. Busy, leave it.
AskingAmber, slow blink. The only state that moves to get your eye.
StalledAmber ring, hollow. Nothing heard for 5 min.
ErroredRed, still.
IdleBlue, still. Turn finished. The Chief of Staff decides if it's waiting on you.
EndedGrey. Sinks to Done.

Taken from the reference. A named pill with an avatar at the top. Each update is a headline, a short paragraph and one card with status rows. A floating dock at the bottom.

Changed for eve. The avatar is the status dot, not a face, so it carries meaning. Cards have actions: Answer, Drop in, Open. Anything the Chief of Staff sends shows a "Sent by" chip and lands in the audit log.

Board. On desktop the needs-you board is a rail beside the thread. On a phone it's the Agents tab, and the badge counts what needs you.

Motion budget. Only two things move: running agents, and an agent asking you something. Everything else is still. Reduced-motion turns all of it off.
