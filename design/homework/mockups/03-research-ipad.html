What changed for 2026

Since January 2025 every German business must be able to receive structured e-invoices 12. What changes in 2026 is the sending side: the grace period for paper and PDF invoices ends on 31 December for firms over €800k turnover, and a year later for everyone else 23. At EU level, ViDA fixes EN 16931 as the common format 1.

What it means for Acme

  • German customers can already reject Acme's PDF invoices. Your export sends PDF only 4.
  • Adding XRechnung or ZUGFeRD output covers all 38 German accounts in the billing notes.
  • One reseller said they'd need Peppol delivery by Q2 6.
